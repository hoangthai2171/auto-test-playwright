<objective>
Cache visible screen snapshots across repeated navigation retries without caching live DOM handles or allowing stale data to cross screen boundaries.

Purpose: remove repeated identical DOM scans introduced by bounded focus/content retry loops while keeping focus and route changes authoritative.
Output: a small immutable snapshot cache, integrated invalidation hooks, and deterministic tests.
</objective>

<decision_coverage>
- D-04: Reuse snapshots within one navigation step or retry loop and invalidate after state changes.
- D-05: Explicitly invalidate after remote actions, route changes, and known content refreshes; do not use a global MutationObserver.
- D-06: Store only minimal immutable records and never cache DOM nodes or Locator handles.
- D-07: Require route/hash and scoped-container identity before reuse; explicit invalidation remains authoritative.
</decision_coverage>

Replace the highest-cost broad DOM enumeration paths with a shared scoped scanner and minimal attribute extraction while preserving current content-row, service, search, and selector-contract behavior.

Purpose: establish the performance-safe discovery primitive that later snapshot and Locator work can reuse.

- D-03: Empty existing roots do not trigger fallback; bounded readiness/retry handles delayed content.
</decision_coverage>
